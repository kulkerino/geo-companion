# Ethics boundary

- This project teaches geographic reasoning; it does not claim to identify a
  location from an image with certainty.
- Source, creator, URL, access date, license, attribution, geographic scope,
  review date, and rights status are required for educational content.
- Only content marked `cleared` may enter active packs, model evidence, or
  training. Public availability is not permission.
- The optional GeoGuessr adapter is post-guess only. It must fail closed for
  unsupported, competitive, ambiguous, or pre-result states and must not
  provide clues or contact the backend during an active round.
- Learner guesses affect review scheduling only; they never overwrite visual
  ground truth.
